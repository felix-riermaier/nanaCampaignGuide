About this guide

A step-by-step route through the Path of Exile 1 campaign, Acts 1 to 10. It isn't tied to a class or build.

What it is

  • An efficient route: most quests that don't give anything essential are skipped.
  • Some quest rewards are picked up later on purpose, on a town visit you make anyway, instead of going back for them right away.
  • Essentially how I run my own campaigns.

What it isn't

  • Not a speedrun route: it doesn't use every portal trick there is.
  • Not a complete quest guide: optional content only shows up if you pick it in Route settings.
  • Not a build guide: rewards are listed generically (Skill Gem, Passive Point), not which gem to take.

How to use it

  • The big button at the bottom of the card completes the step Space. In town, Shift+Space also ticks all town tasks.
  • Click a town task to tick it. Tasks you leave open show up as "Missed" until you tick them.
  • Back undoes the last step Backspace. "Show all steps" lists everything, e.g. to fix a misclick.
  • Route settings chooses optional detours like the Den or which bandit to help.
  • Progress is saved in this browser only.
